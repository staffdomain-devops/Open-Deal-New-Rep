## Change log v1.0 → v1.1

1. **Sequence is now 7 touches:** 5 emails + 2 call tasks (Day 7 and Day 17), on a Day 1 / 5 / 7 / 10 / 15 / 17 / 21 cadence. Touch numbering "X of 7" is the convention everywhere. Email content, order, and day placement are unchanged.
2. **Two new generated deliverables per contact:** Call Task 1 note (§6.1) and Call Task 2 note (§6.2), generated by the same Sonnet call, written as internal-facing SDR briefings. Format inherited from Lane B (LOCKED, JP approved 18 Aug 2026) with the Lane A intro-call adaptation.
3. **One new generated deliverable per contact:** the pinned contact-record note (§6.3), giving the new account owner campaign context and the handover story on the record card. Created and pinned at enrolment.
4. **Internal/external information boundary formalised (inherited from Lane B v2.1):** sensitive outcomes (competitor names, price objections, prior bad experiences, non-response reasons) remain banned from all emails, but MUST surface in the call notes and pin under HOW IT ENDED, paired with a DO NOT SAY line. The rep knowing is an asset; the rep saying it is the crime. This matters MORE in Lane A: the new owner has no lived memory of the account — the notes are everything they know.
5. **Geography module replaced** with the Lane B v2.1 §3.6 module in full (resolution ladder, per-geo copy rules, timezone rules for call-task due times). Same list, same census, same rules — do not maintain two versions.
6. Output JSON schema, lint checks 13–18, write-back mechanics, edge-case matrix, and launch checklist updated accordingly.

## REPLACES §1, second paragraph only — what the pipeline generates

The pipeline generates **8 deliverables per contact**: 5 emails (subject + body, written into the ten contact properties `email_1_subject` … `email_5_body`, unchanged), two call-task notes, and one pinned contact note. A HubSpot sequence sends the emails on the Day 1 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 21 cadence as before; the call tasks are created at enrolment with due dates Day 7 and Day 17; the pin is created and pinned at enrolment. The sequence remains rep-sent. Everything — typed or spoken — runs off the same verified facts from the same brief.

The seven touches: E1 (Day 1) is the handover introduction; E2 (Day 5) gives a case study; **Call 1 (Day 7) is the live handshake behind E1 — the new owner introducing themselves in person, no pitch**; E3 (Day 10) asks about their world; E4 (Day 15) delivers the what's-changed news; **Call 2 (Day 17) attempts the booking**; E5 (Day 21) makes the one ask and steps back.

## NEW §6.1 — Call Task 1 (Day 7): the introduction call

**Placement:** after E2 lands, before E3. **Register:** warm, live, no pitch — the spoken twin of E1. **Lane A framing (the one deliberate difference from Lane B §6.6):** the caller has NEVER spoken to this person. This is not a reconnect; it is the live handshake behind the handover email. Everything the caller knows comes from the file, and the note is written so a rep with zero memory of the account can dial confidently.

The note below is the entire body of a HubSpot task (type CALL, assigned to the contact owner, due Day 7 per §3.6.4), plain text, ~100 words hard cap, generated per-contact.

```
WHY THIS CALL: Intro call, sequence touch 3 of 7. Warm, no pitch. You have
never spoken to [first name] — this is the live handshake behind E1.
TIMEZONE: [only when non-AEST-adjacent — see §3.6.4]
WHO: [name, role, company, one-line context]
HISTORY: [previous rep's name + when they last actually made contact + what
the original conversation was about, one line]
HOW IT ENDED: [real outcome, including anything the emails held back]
DO NOT SAY: [anything on record that must never be voiced — omit line if none]
EMAILS SO FAR: E1 (handover from [previous rep first name]), E2 ([case study
name]). [Reply status.]
GOAL: [the single thing this call is for — usually the E3 question, asked live]
IF VOICEMAIL: First name, "wanted to introduce myself properly rather than
keep emailing," no detail.
```

Rules (all inherited from Lane B §6.6 unless marked Lane A):
- HOW IT ENDED carries the truth, always — this is the one place (with Call 2 and the pin) where INTERNAL - NEVER REFERENCE material appears, paired with DO NOT SAY.
- **Lane A:** HISTORY must name the previous rep, because that is the caller's only bridge into the relationship ("I know Emmanuelle spoke with you about…").
- **Lane A:** the voicemail line is "wanted to introduce myself properly" — never "catch up properly," which implies prior contact the caller does not have. Lint check 15 enforces this.
- GOAL for Call 1 deliberately mirrors E3's question. **Connect rule (LOCKED intent):** if Call 1 connects and the E3 question gets answered live, the rep skips E3 or rewrites its opening line before send. The pin carries this rule so it survives handoffs.
- Voicemail line never references the old deal, the news, or Staff Domain's offer.

## NEW §6.2 — Call Task 2 (Day 17): the booking attempt

**Placement:** after E4 lands, before E5. **Register:** direct, the news is out, the ask is live. Identical to Lane B §6.7 — by Day 17 the new owner has sent four emails and made one call; the introduction premise is spent and the mechanics are the same in both lanes. Same structural rules, caps, and timezone handling as §6.1.

```
WHY THIS CALL: Close attempt, sequence touch 6 of 7. E4 just delivered the
what's-changed news.
TIMEZONE: [as §6.1]
WHO: [name, role, company, location]
HISTORY: [old deal, when, how far it got]
HOW IT ENDED: [real outcome, truth included]
DO NOT SAY: [omit if none]
EMAILS SO FAR: E1 (handover from [previous rep]), E2 ([case study]), E3
([angle]), E4 (the news: scoped in one call, candidates in 1-2 weeks, live
in ~4). [Reply status.]
GOAL: Book the 15 minutes. Offer to bring [role-specific] profiles to the
call. E5 fires Day 21 either way.
IF VOICEMAIL: Reference E4 briefly — "sent through what's changed, worth 15
minutes."
```

## NEW §6.3 — The pinned contact-record note

**Purpose:** the new account owner's 15-second orientation, pinned to the top of the contact record at enrolment. In Lane A this is load-bearing in a way it isn't in Lane B: the sender has no memory of this account. The pin is the difference between a rep who sounds like they've read the file and a rep who proves the handover email was a mail merge. Plain text, ~130 words hard cap, generated per-contact by the same call.

```
=== LANE A RE-ENGAGEMENT (OWNER CHANGED) — READ BEFORE ANY TOUCH ===
WHY: Past prospect, real conversations, never became a client. [Previous rep
first name] owned this account and has left the business. You are the new
owner introducing yourself. Warm handover, not cold outbound.
STORY: [role/company shape, role(s) discussed, key colleagues, one or two
lines]
HOW IT ENDED: [real outcome, truth included]
DO NOT SAY: [omit line if none]
SEQUENCE (enrolled [date]): E1 D1 · E2 D5 · Call D7 · E3 D10 · E4 D15 ·
Call D17 · E5 D21.
RULES: Any reply kills the sequence — respond personally and close open call
tasks. If Call 1 connects, skip or rewrite E3 before it sends. Never reference
their silence or apologise for the gap. Say "dedicated team / someone who
works only for you", never offshoring or BPO.
```

Notes:
- The RULES block is fixed boilerplate, identical to Lane B (code-appended is acceptable; the model may also emit it verbatim — lint check 16 verifies it either way).
- **Lane A:** the WHY line must name the previous rep and state they have left — this is the premise E1 makes to the prospect, and the pin is where the premise is anchored for the rep.
- Records that fail E1 (same owner throughout) do not get a Lane A pin because they do not get generated in this lane at all; they route to Lane B or the live-outreach bucket per the v1.0 exclusion table. (The adapted sequences in the 18 Aug sample batch pre-date this rule and stand as a one-off.)
- Pinning mechanics: created via the engagements API at enrolment; **API support for pinning to be verified at pilot** — fallback is a one-time manual pin pass on the pilot batch. Launch checklist item 14.

## AMENDS §5.2 — system prompt delta

Three changes to the fenced system prompt. Do not retype the prompt; apply these as surgical edits and re-version it.

**(a) Replace the OUTPUT block's JSON schema** with the 8-key schema from §2 above, and append:

```
call1, call2 and pin are INTERNAL notes for the salesperson, never seen by the
recipient. Write them in plain telegraphic English following the exact labelled
formats given in the brief. They are the one place the record's real outcome
must appear, including anything marked INTERNAL - NEVER REFERENCE: state it
plainly under HOW IT ENDED, and when you do, you must also write a DO NOT SAY
line telling the salesperson what can never be voiced and what the on-record
phrasing is. The five emails remain absolutely forbidden from carrying that
material. The salesperson has never spoken to this contact: the previous rep
has left, and everything the salesperson knows comes from these notes, so
HISTORY must name the previous rep and anchor when contact last happened.
Call notes: 100 words maximum each. Pin note: 130 words maximum. No greeting,
no sign-off, no persuasion — these are briefings, not emails.
```

**(b) Amend the INTERNAL - NEVER REFERENCE rule** in HARD MECHANICAL RULES from "Never quote or paraphrase anything the brief marks INTERNAL - NEVER REFERENCE" to "Never quote or paraphrase anything the brief marks INTERNAL - NEVER REFERENCE **in any email**; in call1, call2 and pin it must appear, per the OUTPUT rules."

**(c) Replace the two geography sentences** in HARD MECHANICAL RULES with the Lane B v2.1 §7(c) block verbatim:

```
- The brief resolves COUNTRY to AU, NZ, US or UK. AU: Australian context is
  assumed, never stated. NZ, US, UK: nothing may assume Australia; market
  observations stay neutral. US records: avoid dialect-marked spellings
  entirely (write around them; say "two weeks", never "fortnight").
  Australian English spelling everywhere else.
```

## AMENDS §7 — lint additions

Checks 13–18 (hard unless noted), joining 1–12 from v1.0:

13. **Schema:** output missing any of `call1`, `call2`, `pin`, or any of the three exceeding word caps (100/100/130).
14. **Structure:** call notes missing any required label (WHY THIS CALL / WHO / HISTORY / HOW IT ENDED / EMAILS SO FAR / GOAL / IF VOICEMAIL); pin missing WHY / STORY / HOW IT ENDED / SEQUENCE / RULES; call1 HISTORY or pin WHY missing the previous rep's first name (the brief always carries it — this is the invention detector's inverse: the one name that MUST appear).
15. **Lane A voicemail framing:** call1 IF VOICEMAIL containing "catch up" or any phrasing implying prior contact; must carry the "introduce myself" line. Any "touch X of 7" string where X ≠ 3 in call1 or X ≠ 6 in call2 also fails here.
16. **Sensitive-material direction:** if the brief carries INTERNAL - NEVER REFERENCE content, (a) any email containing it fails — unchanged — AND (b) call1, call2 and pin must each carry a DO NOT SAY line; absence fails. Pin RULES block must match boilerplate.
17. **Timezone line:** US or UK record whose call notes lack the TIMEZONE warning line (per §3.6.4).
18. **Sequence map:** pin's SEQUENCE line must read exactly `E1 D1 · E2 D5 · Call D7 · E3 D10 · E4 D15 · Call D17 · E5 D21.`

Lint check 6 (URL whitelist) extends to call1, call2 and pin: zero URLs, zero placeholder brackets other than the labelled-format brackets shown in the templates, which are filled by generation, never emitted literally.

## AMENDS §8 — write-back

- Email property write-back unchanged (10 properties, multi-line text, batch by `hs_object_id`).
- **Per contact, additionally:** (1) create two task engagements — type CALL, associated to the contact, assigned to `hubspot_owner_id`, `hs_task_subject` = `Lane A Call 1 — {firstname} {lastname} — touch 3 of 7` / `Lane A Call 2 — … — touch 6 of 7`, `hs_task_body` = the generated note, due date = enrolment date + 7 / + 17 days at a §3.6.4-compliant recipient-local time; (2) create the note engagement with the pin body, timestamped at enrolment, then pin.
- **Sender binding extends to tasks and the pin:** in Lane A the current `hubspot_owner_id` IS the new owner the emails sign as, so tasks assigned to anyone else are the same premise failure as a wrong sender — hard rule. If ownership changes between generation and enrolment, regenerate; do not reassign.
- Reply-kill workflow: on any inbound reply, the sequence unenrols (HubSpot native) AND open Lane A call tasks for that contact must be completed/closed — build as a workflow or a daily sweep; verify at pilot.

## AMENDS §9 — edge-case matrix additions

| Situation | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Call 1 connects before E3 sends | Rep skips E3 or rewrites its opening; rule lives in the pin |
| Prospect answers Call 2, no booking, asks for time | Rep judgement: let E5 fire as the graceful step-back, or unenrol — default is let E5 fire |
| Prospect asks on Call 1 "what happened to [previous rep]?" | "[First name]'s moved on from the business" and move forward — never detail, never speculate. Line lives in the rep enablement doc, not the generated notes |
| Contact themselves has left their company (discovered on call or in notes) | Do not proceed; unenrol, flag for a colleague-contact review at the company. Add a contact-departure check to Stage 1 (see checklist 17) |
| Country unresolved after the §3.6.1 ladder | Hold from generation, data-error report |
| US/UK record | Generate normally; call tasks carry the timezone warning; booking-hours mismatch is the rep's call |
| Ownership changes between generation and enrolment | Regenerate against the new owner; never reassign tasks or send stale copy |
